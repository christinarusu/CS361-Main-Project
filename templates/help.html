Need Help? Here Are Some Tips:

To purchase a bag:

  1. Go to home page and browse, search, or filter for the bag you are looking for
  2. Click on the bag image to see more details about the bag. If you want to purchase it, click the green buy button
  3. You will now be on the Buy Bags page. Input your information and click "Purchase"
  4. Confirm that you want to purchase the bag in the window that pops up

To View Orders:

  1. You can now view your purchased bag by going to the "My Account" Page and clicking the "View Order History" button from there
